One record from three devices.

Wear any of them, or all three. With one device Telomy works; with none, your lab history and scores still stand.

Chest patch

The clinical layer

  • ECG and rhythm
  • Gold-standard HRV
  • Sleep stages, breathing
  • Records 24 h on its own

Smart ring

The always-on layer

  • Heart rate every few seconds at night
  • SpO₂ and skin temperature
  • Movement, for sleep detection
  • Logs to its own memory

Apple Health

The everyday layer

  • Steps and workouts
  • VO₂max and resting heart rate
  • Read only, never written

When two devices measure the same thing, the more accurate one is used and the other is kept for comparison. Heart rhythm and HRV come from the patch first, oxygen and temperature from the ring.

Honest about what a sensor can know.

A health product earns trust by saying where its numbers come from and where they stop.

What we validate

  • Sleep staging trained on 151 hospital sleep-lab nights with expert scoring.
  • Rhythm screening trained on 8,528 single-lead ECGs from the PhysioNet challenge.
  • Biomarker models tested on held-out people from the US national health survey, never on the data they learned from.
  • Every chart links to the method and the papers behind it.

What we will not claim

  • Blood pressure and glucose from a ring are estimates the hardware cannot measure. Telomy never shows them as readings.
  • A single-lead ECG screens; it does not diagnose. Repeated flags mean a 12-lead ECG with your doctor.
  • Cholesterol and liver enzymes cannot be predicted from a wearable. Telomy asks for the blood test instead.
  • Your data is stored in India, locked behind your password, shared only with the consent you give.
