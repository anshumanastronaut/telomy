# Longevity & Wellness Interventions — evidence grades + should Telomy implement?

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · Honest, sourced assessment of the interventions in demand (GLP-1, plasma
exchange, EBOO, rapamycin, senolytics, NAD⁺, peptides, HBOT…) and a clear stance for each.

## Telomy's guiding principle
Telomy is a **measurement + clinician-in-the-loop** platform. We do **not administer or prescribe**
anything ourselves. Our differentiated, safe and defensible play is the **data**: for any intervention
a user undertakes (with their own clinician), track its effect on **their own** aging clocks,
biomarkers, disease-risk and wearable signals as an **n-of-1** — nobody else closes that loop at
consumer scale, and it reuses the therapy n-of-1 engine we already have. Three stances:
- **SUPPORT** — evidence-based; screen candidacy + monitor response; a clinician prescribes.
- **TRACK/EDUCATE** — unproven-but-plausible or low-harm; log it, show the person's response, grade the
  evidence honestly; no endorsement.
- **DO NOT OFFER** — weak/absent evidence and real risk or regulatory barriers; educate + warn only.

## The interventions

| Intervention | Evidence (2024–26) | Risk / regulatory | **Telomy stance** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GLP-1 (semaglutide, tirzepatide)** | **Strong.** SELECT: ~20% MACE reduction in obesity *without* diabetes; semaglutide ~29% MACE in meta-analysis; large weight/metabolic benefit. | Rx-only; GI effects, cost, muscle-loss concern | **SUPPORT** — screen candidacy (BMI, metabolic, CVD risk from our models), clinician prescribes, we monitor weight/HbA1c/lipids/**muscular-age** (guard against lean-mass loss). Huge India T2D/obesity market. |
| **HRT / TRT** | Legitimate for documented deficiency; not a general longevity drug. | Rx-only; CV/prostate monitoring | **SUPPORT** — screen (labs), clinician-gated, monitor. |
| **Rapamycin (low-dose)** | **Weak in humans.** PEARL RCT: primary endpoint (visceral fat) negative; 2025 review — insufficient evidence in healthy adults. | Off-label; immune/metabolic effects | **TRACK/EDUCATE** — research-stage; no offer; if a user's clinician prescribes, track response. |
| **Metformin (TAME)** | Unproven for longevity; TAME trial ongoing. | Rx; generally safe | **TRACK/EDUCATE.** |
| **Senolytics (dasatinib+quercetin, fisetin)** | **Early**, disease-specific (fibrosis, CKD); no healthy-aging proof. | D+Q is chemo-derived; real toxicity | **DO NOT OFFER** (D+Q); track/educate only. |
| **NAD⁺ precursors (NR, NMN)** | Reliably raise NAD⁺; **health benefit unproven**. | Supplement; low harm | **TRACK/EDUCATE** — low-harm; show the person's biomarker response. |
| **Peptides (BPC-157, etc.)** | Minimal human evidence; many unapproved. | Unregulated supply, legal grey area | **DO NOT OFFER**; caution. |
| **Therapeutic plasma exchange / "young plasma"** | **Mixed/weak.** Buck Institute 2025: ~1.3y bio-age drop (2.6y with IVIG) — but a donor crossover RCT found **no** epigenetic-age change; young-plasma Alzheimer's trials inconclusive. | FDA-cleared only for specific autoimmune/neuro conditions; **investigational for aging**; procedural risks (access, BP, electrolytes), donor-supply ethics | **DO NOT OFFER / ADMINISTER.** Educate with the honest split evidence + risks; if a user does it elsewhere, track their clock/biomarker response as n-of-1. |
| **EBOO / ozone therapy** | **No credible evidence** for longevity. | Safety concerns; not approved | **DO NOT OFFER.** Educate + warn. |
| **Hyperbaric oxygen (HBOT)** | Evidence for specific clinical indications; longevity claims weak/preliminary. | Approved for defined indications only | **TRACK/EDUCATE.** |

## What this means we actually build (reusing existing assets)
1. **Candidacy + monitoring for the evidence-based drugs (GLP-1, HRT):** our disease/risk models + clocks
   screen who may benefit; the clinician prescribes; we track response (incl. **muscular-age** to flag
   GLP-1 lean-mass loss — a real differentiator). Rx stays with a signed prescription (existing rule).
2. **Intervention n-of-1 tracker:** extend the therapy n-of-1 engine so any logged intervention shows
   its effect on the user's own biomarkers/clocks/wearables with an honest verdict (helped / no signal /
   too early) — the safe, unique data play across *all* the rows above.
3. **Evidence-graded education module:** each intervention carries its grade + sources + a safety note,
   so users get the truth, not hype. This is a trust/brand asset competitors (who sell these services)
   structurally can't match.

## What we must NOT do
Administer or sell plasma exchange, EBOO/ozone, peptides, or unproven infusions; make longevity/lifespan
claims for anything (no therapy is proven to extend human lifespan in rigorous trials); or bypass the
clinician for any prescription.
