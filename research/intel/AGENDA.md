# Telomy Research-Intelligence Agenda (15-day autonomous sweep)

A rotating daily research plan. The scheduled agent picks the next theme not yet covered in
FINDINGS.md, researches it with web search, and appends a dated, sourced entry. Goal: find the
datasets, models, exposures, papers, competitors, regulatory facts and openings that let Telomy
stand out. Each entry must end with **"Standout opportunities for Telomy"**.

| Day | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1 | Competitive landscape — longevity / aging-clock / digital-health companies; what they ship; gaps |
| 2 | Open datasets — new aging, biomarker, disease, wearable, Indian-cohort datasets (links + access) |
| 3 | Aging-clock SOTA — blood/methylation/proteomic/organ clocks, benchmarks, MAE/C-index |
| 4 | Wearable cardiac models — ECG/PPG for AF, MI, structural heart disease; PPG foundation models |
| 5 | Fall detection + emergency alerting — real-fall datasets (FARSEEING), false-alarm-per-day methods |
| 6 | Long-term disease/mortality prediction — HRV/SpO2/RHR multiparameter, time-series foundation models |
| 7 | SLM / medical LLM — new small bases, on-device, tool-grounding, faithfulness eval, agents |
| 8 | Regulatory & market — CDSCO/FDA SaMD, India DPDP, reimbursement, TAM/SAM for India + global |
| 9 | Exposome & lifestyle — environmental exposures, air quality, diet, microbiome links to aging |
| 10 | Nutrition & food recognition — Indian recipe/portion datasets, food-image SOTA |
| 11 | Genomics — Indian population genomics, pharmacogenomics, polygenic risk scores |
| 12 | Sensor hardware — rings/patches, cuffless BP, glucose, new biosensors, validation studies |
| 13 | Funding & grants — who's raising, valuations, longevity VCs, Indian/foreign grants |
| 14 | IP & patents — relevant patents, freedom-to-operate signals, white space |
| 15 | SYNTHESIS — consolidate the fortnight into a "How Telomy stands out" strategy memo |

After day 15 the agent repeats from day 1 with a "what changed / what's new" lens until disabled.
