What Telomy is

A single platform that spans the whole health loop — the sensors that measure you, the AI that explains you, the doctor who treats you, and the therapy that changes you — then measures again. Every competitor owns one slice of that loop. Telomy owns the loop, and serves both the consumer and the clinician on the same data.

① Longevity super-app (consumer)

Lab import + biomarker dashboards, a composite Telomy Score + domain sub-scores, biological-age clocks (blood / muscle / wearable), derived biomarker ratios, plain-language explainers, an evidence-graded protocol recommender, and a closed-loop retest plan.

② Wearables & devices

A smart ECG patch, a smart ring, camera-based HRV, Apple Health, and overnight multi-sensor capture — fused into one longitudinal record.

③ Sinc — the AI health copilot

Runs on-device (SLM) and in the cloud (Claude), grounded in your own data, with document & image upload. Private, offline-capable, cost-efficient.

④ Clinical engine + doctor platform

An OpenEvidence/Vera-class retrieve→grade→cited-answer engine, point-of-care calculators and an FDA-label drug reference, on a multi-tenant care graph with diagnostics / therapy / Rx workflows for doctors & diagnostic centres.

Plus an owned hardware ecosystem (Bonphul: HBOT, cryotherapy, oxygen, red-light) that closes the loop into physical therapy delivery and into clinics.

What we offer — the full feature set

For the member (longevity super-app)

Telomy Score + biological age

One 0–100 number from 7 longevity-weighted domains (cardiovascular, metabolic, inflammation, kidney, liver, nutrition, hormones), plus a bio-age readout from the best available clock vs your actual age — weakest domains first, so you know what to work on.

Lab insights — derived ratios

TyG, METS-IR, ApoB & atherogenic ratios, non-HDL, remnant cholesterol, AIP, HOMA-IR, HbA1c→eAG — higher-signal than any single marker, each with its optimal band and primary citation.

Plain-language explainers + optimal-vs-normal

Every biomarker explained in plain words, with the optimal band (not just the lab-normal range) and your trend across draws.

Closed-loop retest

Measure → act → re-measure. We prompt the right retest cadence (sooner for out-of-range markers) and show what improved or moved the wrong way since last time.

Protocols to discuss

Evidence-graded lifestyle & supplement levers matched to your biomarkers; prescription/hormone/peptide options are clinician-gated — shown by name only and routed to your doctor, never auto-recommended.

Camera-HRV & device capture

A 60-second camera heart-rate/HRV read (validated on-device), plus the ECG patch, smart ring and Apple Health, with overnight capture.

Sinc — the AI copilot

  • Grounded in your own vault: answers cite your real numbers and never invent them (a numeric-grounding guard).
  • Runs on-device (small language model) for privacy & offline, and in the cloud (Claude) for depth — including a camera/vision + document upload (PDFs, docs, images).
  • Deep-research mode with citations; turns results into next steps you sign off on.

For doctors & diagnostic centres (clinical platform)

Clinical evidence engine

Retrieve → grade → cited answer over PubMed, FDA labels and guidelines — with an explicit evidence-grade badge per source and a plain-language, point-of-care answer. Decision support, never a directive.

Calculators & drug reference

15+ validated point-of-care scores (CHA₂DS₂-VASc, CURB-65, CKD-EPI, FIB-4, MELD-Na…) and an FDA-label drug reference (indications, dosing, interactions, real-world adverse-event signals).

Patient-grounded answers

The same engine answers the doctor's question grounded in the patient's own biomarkers & care graph — a capability pure clinical-AI tools don't have.

Care graph & workflows

Multi-tenant care graph, care-review queue, therapy & Rx engines, consults and diagnostic-centre workspaces — the clinician and the consumer on one platform.

Ecosystem: Bonphul hardware (HBOT, cryo, O₂, red-light) + a knowledge base built on open corpora (PMC-OA, DailyMed, USPSTF/CDC) and India sources (ICMR, MoHFW, CDSCO, IFCT foods) with grade + license + region on every chunk.

What keeps us ahead — the USPs

  1. The full-stack closed loop no point solution owns. Device → data → AI → doctor → therapy → re-measure. Every rival owns one or two links; Telomy owns the loop end-to-end — and it's built, not slideware.
  2. Clinician + consumer on one care graph. Superpower/Function have no real doctor platform; OpenEvidence/Vera have no patient data. Telomy's engine answers the doctor's question on the patient's own numbers. No one else spans both sides.
  3. Evidence you can see the grade of. Cited, graded answers with an explicit grade badge — beating the clinical-AI leaders (Vera hides its scale; OpenEvidence is ad-funded). "We show you the grade, not just the answer."
  4. On-device + cloud AI. Privacy-preserving, offline-capable, cost-efficient — and a genuine DPDP / data-localisation asset. No consumer-longevity rival runs on-device.
  5. Measure → act → prove. The closed-loop retest turns a one-off test into a lifelong, measurable, retention-driving loop — and a proof-of-impact story.
  6. India depth. Indian foods (IFCT), ICMR/CDSCO/MoHFW guidelines, Jan-Aushadhi affordability, NHANES/India-aware models, vernacular, DPDP-native — years of local data work US incumbents can't replicate.
  7. Owned hardware (Bonphul) closes the loop into physical therapy and clinics. Instant, cheap bio-age from the panel you already have vs $299–499 / 3–6-week methylation tests.

The moat

Data network effects

Multi-device longitudinal data (patch + ring + camera-HRV + overnight + labs + wearables) compounds per user; India datasets (IFCT, IndiGenomes, ICMR, NHANES/BRFSS-trained models) are a corpus rivals can't buy. Every retest and device-night makes the models better and the user stickier.

Two-sided care-graph network

Doctors bring patients; patients bring doctors; diagnostic centres anchor both (B2B2C). Classic two-sided lock-in that single-sided rivals cannot replicate.

On-device models

A real engineering moat — privacy, offline, cost — hard for cloud-only rivals to retrofit, and DPDP-aligned.

Validation + vertical integration

Published model error bars, a non-device-CDS posture, CDSCO/DPDP compliance-by-design, and owned hardware — slow to build, slow to copy.

Be honest about what's copyable: dashboards, derived ratios and a composite score are table-stakes within a year — parity features, not moats. The edge is the grade badge + patient-context grounding + India corpus + the two-sided data loop, not the RAG itself. Deepen the four compounding moats, not the feature list.
