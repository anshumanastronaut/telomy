# Superpower (superpower.com) — feature teardown + "bring to Telomy" plan

**Target confirmed:** Superpower Health, Inc. — US consumer preventive/longevity-health membership at superpower.com. Founded 2023 (Jacob Peters CEO, Max Marchione, Kevin Unkrich; CLO Dr. Anant Vinjamoori). Not the Claude "superpowers" skill system.

**Caveats to carry forward:** (1) pricing varies across pages ($199 current vs $499 historical vs $349 deeper tier) — verify live before quoting; (2) **"peptide engine" is NOT a documented product — do not represent it as one**; (3) **no clinical outcome data** exists for their peptide protocols; (4) wearable/Apple Health integration only partially verifiable; (5) a third-party "Peptide Friday"/thymosin-alpha-1 anecdote is unverified.

## 1. What it is & positioning
Annual membership around comprehensive blood biomarker testing + an app that turns results into a personalized plan, an AI assistant, a human care team, and a marketplace (supplements, Rx, peptides). Pitched as the "world's first health super-app," reactive→proactive.
- **Price (2026):** **$199/yr** (~$0.55/day), HSA/FSA eligible; earlier press $499/yr biannual; a ~$349 150-biomarker tier seen; GLP-1/peptides are separate paid add-ons.
- **Get:** 100+ biomarkers from one draw; biological age; **17 precision health scores + a composite "Superpower Score"**; personalized action plan; **Superpower AI** (24/7 on your data); hybrid AI+human concierge (24h weekday); results ~10 days; 2,000+ partner labs (Quest) or at-home draw (extra); marketplace (discounted Rx/peptides/supplements, add-ons: gut microbiome, toxins, Grail cancer screen).
- **Target:** health-optimizer/longevity-curious US consumer, cash-pay; celebrity-marketed.
- **vs competitors:** vs **Function Health** ($499, 100+ markers 2×/yr) — undercuts on price, leans harder on AI+care team+marketplace/treatment (wants to *act on* results); criticism that Function's base panel is deeper. vs **InsideTracker** broader+concierge. vs **Levels** (CGM niche) whole-panel. vs **Marek Health** (clinician hormone/peptide) — same treatment territory but mass-market consumer app ("Marek productized").

## 2. Every product feature & screen (input→output)
1. **Onboarding/booking** — signup, questionnaire, consent, choose at-home vs lab → physician-authorized requisition. US-only, 18+.
2. **Blood draw** — ~15 min (fasting some markers) → CLIA/CAP lab.
3. **Results dashboard** — ~10 days → all labs/meds/history in one place; **plain-language explanation of every biomarker** ("no jargon, no PDFs"); SMS alert; **records import** (upload past records/PDFs). UI widely praised.
4. **Biomarker panel** — 100+ across ~21 categories: heart/vascular (ApoB, Lp(a), lipid + derived ratios — atherogenic indices, TyG, METS-IR), metabolic, liver, kidney (urinalysis, cystatin C, eGFR), inflammation/immune (hs-CRP, ESR, ANA/anti-CCP/dsDNA, IgE), hormones/thyroid, nutrients, energy/mitochondrial (organic acids), **brain (ApoE genotype, Aβ42/40, p-tau217, homocysteine)**, gut (tTG-Ab, "digestive organ age"), DNA/longevity, add-ons (microbiome, toxins, Grail). **Many are derived ratios/indices computed from base labs — cheap, high-signal.**
5. **Biological age + scores** — bio-age from the panel; 17 precision scores; composite Superpower Score; annual report; tracked across retests.
6. **Personalized action plan / protocols** — results+goals+lifestyle(+records/wearables/genetics) → step-by-step plan (diet/fitness/supplements/treatments) incl. custom supplement plan. **Closed-loop:** intervene → retest 3–6 mo → measure → adjust.
7. **Superpower AI** — 24/7 assistant on member's data; results→next steps "based on current research"; does NOT replace clinician for prescribing.
8. **Care team / concierge (hybrid AI+human)** — clinician messaging, no appointment; ~24h weekday; physicians authorize tests + all Rx.
9. **Retesting cadence** — 1 comprehensive/yr included; complimentary follow-up (~$99 value) at 3–6 mo; extra on demand.
10. **Integrations** — "connected wearable insights" + genetics mentioned (strongest on GLP-1 page); Apple Health not explicitly verified. *(partially verified)*
11. **Marketplace** — discounted Rx, peptides, curated supplements, add-on diagnostics; member-gated pricing.
12. **Medical calculators** — A1C↔eAG, eGFR(MDRD), corrected calcium(Payne), basal insulin.
13. **Family accounts** — not found (gift memberships exist).

## 3. The PEPTIDE program & "engine" (deep-dive)
**Status:** real but in transition + heavily caveated. Main `/peptides` page is a **waitlist**; specific peptides live as marketplace pages + press.
- **GLP-1 program** (`/glp1`, most mature): semaglutide $69/mo, tirzepatide $119/mo, oral tirzepatide $229/mo; clinician messaging, dose reviews, 45+ biomarker panel unlocking day 23, nutrition/strength plans, wearable insights.
- **Marketplace peptides:** sermorelin (inj + troches; GHRH analog — GH/muscle/fat/sleep/cognition), tesamorelin (recomp), SS-31/elamipretide ("energy"/mitochondrial). Press (AOL): launched "US-legal peptides" in Jan — GLP-1, SS-31, sermorelin, tesamorelin.
- **Prescribed flow (verified):** eligibility questionnaire → **licensed Superpower physician review** → compounded product via partner pharmacy + discreet shipping → weekly check-ins, dose adjustments, 24/7 provider access. Personalization framed as tied to biomarker panel + records + closed-loop retest.
- **"Peptide engine":** **NOT an official/verified term.** Nearest real thing = AI biomarker analysis + undisclosed proprietary protocol-recommendation algorithms feeding the action plan. **Treat as marketing/aspirational, not a documented product.**
- **Clinical study/data:** **NONE found.** No peer-reviewed/published outcome study. Language = "pure, third-party tested, backed by science" with no trials/citations. Their own CLO acknowledges GH-axis risks; independent experts say "claims outrun the research."
- **Safety/regulatory (verified):** repeated disclaimer "compounded products are not FDA-approved… FDA does not verify safety/effectiveness/quality of compounded drugs." Standard GLP-1 contraindications stated. A founder+CLO joint statement (Apr 16 2026) welcomes an FDA move giving "regulatory clarity" to "several peptides" (tied in earlier press to an HHS/RFK Jr. announcement on 14 peptides) — **report as announcement, not final rule; partly unverified.**

- **Pricing:** membership $199/yr (was $499 biannual; $349 deeper tier); at-home draw extra; GLP-1 from $69/mo; peptides member-priced/compounded; marketplace à la carte. **Model = subscription funnel + high-margin marketplace/treatment attach** (meds/peptides/supplements/add-on tests).

## 5. Strengths & weaknesses
**Strengths:** aggressive price vs Function; broad panel incl. advanced markers (ApoB, Lp(a), ApoE, p-tau217, cystatin C, organic acids); plain-language UX; bio-age + 17 scores = sticky "number to improve"; closed-loop retest; strong funding/brand/waitlist; **full-stack move into treatment (GLP-1/peptides/Rx + supplements) is a real revenue moat**; cheap derived-ratio "insights."
**Weaknesses (Trustpilot ~4.5/224 + independent):** logistics failures (sample to wrong lab/never processed); **a privacy incident (another member's PHI delivered to a reviewer via mistyped onboarding email) — real trust red flag**; inconsistent customer service; base panel less comprehensive than Function unless you pay up; **peptide/longevity claims outrun evidence, no published outcomes, compounded-drug gray zone**; pricing inconsistency; curated/promotional own-site reviews.

## 6. Prioritized "bring to Telomy" (value × effort)
Legend: **[Have]** · **[Extend]** · **[New]**. Telomy already has: labs import, biomarker dashboards, AI copilot (Sinc on-device+cloud), doctor platform, therapy tracking, wearables.

**Tier 1 — high value, low/med effort (first):**
1. **Biological age + a single composite "Telomy Score" + 10–20 named sub-scores** [Extend] — Superpower's biggest retention hook ("one number to lower" + named scores). We have aging-clock models → surface a headline bio-age + scorecard. Highest value, reuses existing ML.
2. **Derived biomarker ratios/indices engine** [New-ish, low effort] — ApoB ratios, TyG, METS-IR, NLR/SII, atherogenic indices, cystatin-C eGFR, corrected calcium, A1C↔eAG — pure computation on labs we already import. Multiplies "insights" near-zero cost. Fast win.
3. **Plain-language per-biomarker explainer + optimal-vs-normal ranges + trend lines** [Have/Extend] — Sinc auto-explains every marker, shows *optimal* (not just lab-normal) bands + trend across draws.
4. **Closed-loop retest cadence** [Extend] — prompt a 3–6 mo retest tied to each intervention, show before/after — ties labs+therapy+wearables into one loop.

**Tier 2 — high value, med/high effort:**
5. **Hybrid AI+human concierge care team w/ 24h messaging** [Extend] — wire Sinc + doctor platform into a visible async "care team" inbox with SLA.
6. **Records import (upload past labs/records)** [Extend] — table stakes; feeds the AI. *(Sinc file upload just shipped — a foundation.)*
7. **Action plan across lifestyle/supplements/treatments from panel + wearables** [Extend] — add the structured goal-linked plan layer on therapy tracking.
8. **Marketplace / supplement + add-on test attach** [New, business-model] — the real monetization lever; for India curate IFCT-aware supplements + partner lab add-ons.

**Tier 3 — high value, high regulatory risk (sequence carefully):**
9. **Peptide/therapy recommendation engine** [New] — headline competitor feature, genuinely novel, but **do not clone the "peptide engine" branding/claims.** Build as a **clinician-gated** protocol recommender surfacing **evidence-graded** options that **always route through a licensed doctor** on our platform.
10. **GLP-1 / prescription program** [New] — high demand/margin, highest regulatory burden.

**Skip:** cloning "backed by science" peptide marketing; unsubstantiated longevity/efficacy claims; curated testimonials.

### Regulatory / safety flags (critical)
- **India CDSCO:** BPC-157/sermorelin/tesamorelin/SS-31 are **not India-approved consumer products**; compounding/import/prescription tightly controlled. GLP-1s are Rx-only via registered doctors/pharmacies. A peptide recommender is **far riskier in India than the US** → must be clinician-gated, never auto-recommend unapproved substances, carry explicit disclaimers.
- **India DPDP 2023:** health data sensitive → explicit consent, data-localization awareness, breach notification. **Engineer against Superpower's mis-delivered-PHI failure** (verify onboarding email, strict account isolation — reuse `tenancy.py` isolation + our pytest TELOMY_DATA_DIR isolation discipline).
- **Global:** frame bio-age/scores as wellness/informational (not diagnostic) unless clinically validated; state our aging-clock MAEs honestly (e.g. muscle clock MAE ~9y); copy compounded-drug FDA-style disclaimers if we ever touch Rx.
