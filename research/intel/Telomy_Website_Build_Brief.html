Website Build Brief — art direction, IA, section copy, interaction spec & stack. Prepared 29 Oct 2026. Companion to the Telomy Feature · USP · Moat Dossier.

What this site has to do

Make a first-time visitor understand, in one scroll, that Telomy is the only platform that closes the whole health loop — and feel that it is premium, clinical-grade and alive. The site is the top of every funnel: consumer waitlist/app install, clinician & diagnostic-centre demo requests, and the investor read. It must out-class the comparison set (Superpower, Function, Oura, Whoop, OpenEvidence) on craft, not just claims.

Primary goals

① Member app install / waitlist. ② Clinician & centre "book a demo". ③ Investor credibility. One site, three clear doors.

Who it talks to

The health-curious consumer, the practising doctor / centre owner, and the investor — each needs a different lead, reachable within one click of the hero.

The feeling

Calm, exact, confident, unmistakably medical-grade. Editorial, not SaaS-generic. "A doctor you'd trust, that happens to be beautiful."

Success = the visitor can say what Telomy is in one sentence, has seen one thing no competitor can show (the organ-age twin or the signals-not-verdicts demo), and knows exactly which button is theirs.

Art direction — what makes it standout

A dark, editorial, instrument-grade aesthetic: deep ink canvas, one living accent, serif-and-grotesk typography, and motion that is the product, not decoration. Every page should look like a readout, not a brochure.

Palette

Ink #09130F Ink-2 #10201A Teal #2F8F6B Glow #4FD08F Copper #A9643A Soft #F4F7F5
Dark ink canvas as the default; teal is the single system accent (the only colour that moves/glows); copper is the warm human counter-accent (used sparingly, for the "felt" side and warnings). Light editorial sections for reading-heavy pages.

Typography

TelomySans (brand face) for hero lines only; a refined serif (Fraunces-class) for headlines and big numbers; a grotesk (Geist/Inter) for reading and UI. Tight negative tracking on large serif; tabular numerals everywhere a value appears. One quiet micro-label style (uppercase, tracked).

Motion language

The DNA double-helix loader is the brand's motion signature — reuse its rhythm everywhere (section reveals, the loop, the twin rotation). Motion is slow, exact, confident; nothing bounces. Scroll-driven, respects prefers-reduced-motion, always ≥60fps. If an animation doesn't explain something, cut it.

Imagery & texture

No stock "happy people" photos. Use real product UI (the twin, the dials, the dashboards), real device shots (patch, ring), and abstract data-driven texture (ECG traces, HRV waveforms, the DNA motif) as section dividers. Grain and depth over flat gradients.

House rule: every screen should feel like a precise instrument reading in low light — the opposite of a pastel wellness app. Benchmarks for craft: Oura's product pages, Superpower's typography, Linear's motion discipline, Stripe's structure.

The homepage — section by section

01 · Hero

Headline: "Measure your health. Act on it. Prove it worked." Sub: the one-line positioning. Behind it, the 3D organ-age twin rotating slowly, each organ faintly lit by its own age. Two buttons: Get the app / For clinicians.

The twin is the single most ownable visual Telomy has — it leads, and it moves.

02 · The loop (the core idea)

A scroll-driven animation of Device → Data → AI → Doctor → Therapy → Re-measure as a closing ring. "Every player owns one slice. We own the loop." This is the section that explains the whole company.

03 · Organ-age twin

Interactive: tap an organ, see its mortality-anchored biological age and what moves it. "Not one bio-age — eight, and which one is ageing fastest." The hero feature, shown live.

04 · Signals, not verdicts

A live mini-demo: a sleep score with the person's own normal band, the felt-vs-measured check-in, and two devices disagreeing shown as a range. "Your number is a signal, not a verdict." The trust pillar — and the orthosomnia-era wedge.

05 · One AI, on your own numbers

Sinc answering with cited, grounded answers — on-device + cloud. Show a real grounded answer with citations, and the grade badge. "Evidence you can see the grade of."

06 · For clinicians

A split band: the same evidence engine, answering the doctor's question on the patient's real data. One line, one CTA to the clinician page.

07 · Devices & therapy

Patch, ring, camera-HRV and Bonphul hardware — the loop made physical. Real device photography on ink.

08 · Built for India

The NCD stat (101M diabetic + 136M prediabetic), IFCT foods, DPDP-native, Jan-Aushadhi affordability. "Built for India, ready for the world."

09 · Proof & honesty

Published error bars, the "what we deliberately don't claim" box, validated-against-reference-device numbers. Credibility as a feature.

10 · Closing CTA. Restate the one line, the three doors (member / clinician / investor), and the DNA motif resolving. Footer with legal + DPDP.

Signature interactions — the five things people remember

  1. The living organ-age twin. A real-time 3D model (WebGL/Three.js) where each organ glows by its age; hover/tap reveals the number and its drivers. The homepage hero and the platform page both use it. hero
  2. The closing loop. A scroll-scrubbed SVG/canvas animation where the six stages connect into a ring as you scroll — the company's thesis, felt not read.
  3. The signals-not-verdicts demo. An interactive score that shows a bare number, then reveals the personal band, the felt-vs-measured line, and a device-disagreement range — teaching the philosophy by letting the visitor toggle it.
  4. The grade-badge reveal. A cited clinical answer where each citation flips to show its evidence grade — the one thing OpenEvidence/Vera don't show.
  5. The DNA through-line. The double-helix loader motif recurs as section dividers and the page's scroll progress indicator, tying the whole site into one object.

Each must degrade gracefully: a static, captioned fallback for reduced-motion and for crawlers. Interactions explain the product — never ornament for its own sake.

Design system & components

Tokens

Colour, type scale, spacing (4px base), radius (10–28px), elevation (soft, low-contrast), motion (durations 200/400/800ms, one easing). One source of truth, shared with the app's identity so web and product feel like one brand.

Core components

Nav (sticky, condensing), hero, section-reveal wrapper, feature card, stat/KPI tile, comparison table, score-dial, grade badge, device card, pricing table, quote/testimonial, CTA band, footer, cookie/consent (DPDP-first, decline-by-default).

Content components

Evidence-citation block, "what we don't claim" note, biomarker/marker chip, organ-twin embed, loop animation embed, waitlist form, demo-request form (role-aware).

Responsive

Mobile-first; the twin and loop become tap-through stories on phone. 16px side gutter, no horizontal scroll, type that stays legible at 320px. Dark by default; a tasteful light mode for reading pages.

Build — stack, performance, discoverability

Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind, React Three Fiber for the twin, GSAP/Framer Motion + Lenis for scroll, hosted on Vercel. A lightweight CMS (Sanity/Contentlayer) for Research/Blog so marketing can ship without a deploy. One shared token package with the app.

Performance budget

LCP < 2.0s on 4G mobile, CLS < 0.1, JS < 200KB on first paint (twin/loop lazy-loaded below the fold, with a poster frame). 60fps on all motion. Images AVIF/WebP, responsive, lazy.

SEO + AEO

Semantic HTML, per-page metadata & Open Graph, JSON-LD (Organization, Product, FAQ, MedicalWebPage). Answer-engine-optimised — clean, quotable, source-cited copy so Telomy is the answer when someone asks an AI "best longevity platform in India". The Research hub is the organic surface.

Accessibility & trust

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, focus states, keyboard paths, reduced-motion, captioned media. DPDP-first consent (decline by default, no non-essential cookies pre-consent). Medical disclaimer on every clinical claim. Analytics: privacy-respecting (Plausible-class) + conversion events per door.

Guardrails carried from the dossier: clinical features are decision support, not directives; bio-age and scores are wellness/informational with error bars stated; prescription/hormone/peptide options are clinician-gated; market and competitor figures are analyst/company-reported and must be labelled as such. Nothing on the site should read as a medical or lifespan promise.

Build phases

1
Foundation (week 1–2). Tokens + component library + nav/footer + the two reading templates (Science, About). Deploy the skeleton with real copy from this brief and the dossier.
2
Homepage + the two product pages (week 2–4). All sections with final copy; the loop animation and the signals-not-verdicts demo. Clinician page with the demo-request flow.
3
The twin & signature motion (week 4–6). The live 3D organ-age twin, the grade-badge reveal, the DNA through-line. The craft layer that makes it standout — time-boxed and always with a static fallback.
4
Pricing, Research hub, polish (week 6–7). Pricing with the test menu/bundles, CMS-backed Research for SEO/AEO, full a11y + performance pass, analytics + consent, launch checklist.

Sequence logic: ship a credible, fast, well-written site first (phases 1–2), then layer the signature motion (phase 3) so the site is never blocked on the hardest animation. Copy is ready today — it comes straight from the dossier.
