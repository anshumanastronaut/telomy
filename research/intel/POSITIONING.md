# Telomy — Market, Competitive & Positioning Dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-28. For website, investor pitch and sales. `[est.]` = analyst/company-reported, re-verify before public use. Market-size figures vary 2–5× by definition; ranges + the most defensible central figure are given per number. The safest external claims are the **structural** ones (full-stack loop, two-sided graph, India moat, on-device AI, evidence-grading) — true, built, hard to copy, independent of any contested number.*

## 1. Market landscape & size
**Global:**
- **Longevity (narrow):** ~$27.6–29.8B (2025) → ~$47B by 2031, ~8–9% CAGR (SNS Insider; Mordor/R&M). Most defensible "longevity" headline.
- **Anti-aging (broad):** ~$80B (2025) → $137B by 2035, ~5.5% (Towards Healthcare). Includes cosmetics/devices.
- **Wearable medical devices:** ~$42–45B → ~$76–78B by 2030, ~11–17% (MarketsandMarkets; GIA).
- **Clinical Decision Support (CDSS):** ~$2.5–4B → ~$3.9–9B by 2030–34, ~8–13% (M&M; Towards Healthcare). NLP-in-health adjacent $5.2B→$16B by 2030 @25%.
- **Remote patient monitoring:** ~$25–30B → ~$57–66B by 2030–31, ~12–13% (M&M).

**India (the 70% focus):**
- **India digital health:** ~$7.6B (M&M) to ~$19B (IMARC) in 2025 → $23.3B by 2030, **18–25% CAGR**.
- **India diagnostics/labs:** ~$12.4B (Ken) → ~$23.7B by 2031, ~11.4%; "set to double by 2030." The data layer Telomy sits on.
- **India preventive/wellness:** ~$197B by 2030 (IBEF wellness); "$532B by 2030 @22%" preventive — `[est., verify]`.
- **TAM/SAM framing:** India digital-health TAM ~$7.6–19B (2025) @18–25%; near-term SAM = India diagnostics (~$12.4B) ∩ urban cash-pay preventive consumer + private clinic/diagnostic-centre B2B. Build SAM **bottom-up** (annual-health-check households + addressable private centres/clinicians), not top-down share.

**Tailwinds (quotable):**
- **India NCD burden:** NCDs ~63–66% of all deaths (WHO/ICMR-NCDIR). ICMR-INDIAB 2023 (*Lancet D&E*): **101M diabetic + 136M prediabetic** Indians. India projected to miss WHO 25×25. A young population aging into a metabolic epidemic, under-screened — the strongest India tailwind.
- **Consumer longevity boom:** Function, Superpower, Oura, Whoop, Ultrahuman all scaled 2023–26; Bryan Johnson mainstreamed "bio-age as a number."
- **AI-in-medicine inflection:** **OpenEvidence $12B val (Jan 2026 Series D)**, 40%+ US physicians daily `[company-reported]` — the clinical-AI copilot category is venture-validated.
- **Device+labs convergence:** Whoop and Oura both added blood testing in 2025 — converging on exactly Telomy's device+labs+AI stack.
- **India rails:** ABDM/ABHA, DPDP Act 2023, Jan Aushadhi, government preventive-care push.

## 2. Competitor map & how Telomy differs
*No competitor spans **device → data → AI → doctor → therapy** in one loop. Each owns one layer. Telomy's wedge: the only platform that closes the loop AND serves consumer + clinician on one care graph.*

**Consumer labs/longevity:**
- **Superpower** (US super-app, $199/yr, 100+ markers, bio-age+17 scores, AI assistant, hybrid care team, marketplace; $30M A, 150k waitlist) — no wearables, no on-device AI, a PHI mis-delivery incident, peptide claims outrunning evidence.
- **Function Health** ($365/yr, 160+ markers 2×/yr) — testing+dashboard, light action layer.
- **InsideTracker / Levels / Marek** — blood+DNA optimization / CGM-glucose niche / clinician hormone-peptide. Each single-layer.
- **Blueprint/TruDiagnostic/Elysium/Tally/Zoe/Lifeforce** — single-clock or supplement-commerce; structural conflict (recs lead into own products). Telomy's bio-age is **instant from the cheap panel users already have** vs $299–499 + 3–6 wk methylation.

**Clinical AI (doctor-side moat):**
- **OpenEvidence** ($12B, 40%+ US MDs) — no patient data, no India depth, ad-funded conflict.
- **Vera Health** (YC S24, $32M, 900+ calculators, CME) — **hides its grade scale**; no patient context, no consumer side.
- **UpToDate / Glass Health** — legacy reference / DDx scratchpad.

**Wearables/health-OS:**
- **Oura** (~$11B `[est.]`, ring) / **Whoop** (~$10B `[est.]`, strap) — ring/strap + scores, no labs depth, no clinician platform.
- **Apple Health** — horizontal aggregator; Telomy ingests it as one input.
- **Ultrahuman (India-born)** — ring+CGM+Blood Vision, $70M @ $365M (2026), ~$140M ARR `[est.]`; device-first, consumer-only. Closest India analog — position against directly.

**India platforms:** Eka Care (ABDM records), Practo (teleconsult/booking), HealthifyMe (AI nutrition), Tata 1mg/PharmEasy/Apollo 24|7 (pharmacy+e-diagnostics) — each commerce/access/records-first; none do longevity scoring + bio-age + clinical AI + closed loop.

**One-liner:** *Superpower without the clinical engine; OpenEvidence without the patient data; Oura without the doctor; Ultrahuman without the evidence. Telomy is all of them, on one graph, India-first.*

## 3. Core USPs (ranked)
1. **Full-stack vertical integration — the closed loop no point solution owns.** Device (ECG patch, ring, camera-HRV, overnight) → data → AI (Sinc) → doctor/centre → therapy → **re-measure**. Structurally hard to copy; already built end-to-end.
2. **Clinician + consumer on one care graph.** Same evidence engine answers the *doctor's* question grounded in the *patient's* own biomarkers. No competitor spans both sides.
3. **Evidence-grade transparency (beats clinical-AI leaders).** Retrieve→grade→answer with an **explicit GRADE/Oxford-CEBM badge per claim** + superseded-guideline flags. *"We show you the grade, not just the answer."*
4. **On-device + cloud AI (privacy + offline + cost).** Sinc runs an on-device SLM *and* cloud Claude — DPDP/data-localization asset; no consumer-longevity rival runs on-device.
5. **Closed-loop measure→act→re-measure.** Retest prompts tied to each intervention + before/after → retention engine + proof-of-impact.
6. **India depth (durable moat vs US players).** IFCT 2017 foods, ICMR/CDSCO/MoHFW corpus, Jan Aushadhi affordability, NHANES/IndiGenomes-aware models, vernacular, DPDP-native.
7. **Owned hardware (Bonphul).** HBOT/cryo/O2/red-light — closes the loop into physical therapy + clinics (Mitogenez reference clinic).
8. **Instant cheap bio-age** vs slow expensive methylation.

## 4. Moat
**Defensible (deepen):** data network effects (multi-device longitudinal + India datasets — every retest/device-night improves models & stickiness); two-sided care-graph network (doctors↔patients↔centres); on-device models (privacy+offline+cost, DPDP); regulatory/clinical validation (published MAEs, non-device CDS); vertical integration + Bonphul hardware.
**Easily copied (don't over-index):** dashboards, derived ratios, plain-language explainers, a composite score (Superpower already has it) = parity features; a cited Q&A over open corpora alone (OpenEvidence/Vera well-funded) — Telomy's edge is the **grade badge + patient-context grounding + India corpus**, not the RAG itself.
**Deepen priorities:** (1) longitudinal multi-device data + retest loop; (2) two-sided doctor/centre network; (3) India corpus + validated India models; (4) published validation. These compound; the feature list doesn't.

## 5. Positioning & messaging
**One-liner:** *Telomy is the vertically integrated longevity platform where your devices, your labs, and your doctor meet one AI — to measure your health, act on it, and prove it worked.*
**Investor alt:** *The full-stack preventive-health platform for India and the world — device → data → AI → doctor → therapy, in one closed loop.*

**Pillars:** (1) One loop, not one test. (2) A doctor and an AI on your own numbers. (3) Evidence you can see the grade of. (4) Private by design — on your device. (5) Built for India, ready for the world.

**Elevator (investor):** *"Longevity testing is a $28B+ global market growing ~9%, clinical AI just minted a $12B company in OpenEvidence, and India's digital-health market compounds at 18–25%. Every player owns one slice — Function tests you, Oura tracks you, OpenEvidence answers the doctor — none closes the loop. Telomy spans device, data, AI, doctor and therapy on a single care graph, India-first. We turn a one-off blood test into a lifelong, measurable, clinician-backed loop — and we own the hardware and the clinical evidence engine to deliver it."*

**Taglines:** Measure. Act. Prove. · Your whole health, one loop. · The longevity platform with a doctor inside. · Know your biological age. Then change it.

**Lead by audience:** Consumers → Telomy Score + bio-age ("one number to lower") + the loop. Doctors/centres → cited, graded clinical engine + calculators + drug reference on the patient's own data, multi-tenant. Investors → full-stack integration + two-sided network + India moat, against OpenEvidence $12B / Oura $11B / Superpower-Function comps.

**Do NOT claim (guardrails):** non-device CDS (citations always, no unexplainable directive); no unproven lifespan claims (bio-age = wellness/informational, state MAEs e.g. muscle ~9y); clinician-gated Rx/peptides + FDA-compounding + India CDSCO caveats; label company metrics/FAERS honestly; engineer against PHI mis-delivery (make privacy a proof point).

## 6. Proof points & honest gaps
**Proof points:** built-not-slideware (ratios/score/retest/protocols/KB/calculators/drugref all in the repo); evidence grounding (retrieve→grade→answer over PMC-OA/DailyMed/USPSTF/ICMR with grade+license+region per chunk + citation guard); real multi-device capture (patch/ring/camera-HRV/Apple Health/overnight, validated); validated models with error bars (NHANES clocks, muscle MAE ~9y, chronic risk on BRFSS ~1.3M); India assets (IFCT, ICMR/CDSCO/MoHFW, DPDP tenancy, Bonphul + Mitogenez clinic); guardrails as a feature (non-device CDS, clinician-gated Rx, grade badges, audit log).
**Honest gaps:** A-track knowledge engine infra-gated (bulk/India corpus + dense vectors need the ingest box; prod = FTS5 + live-PubMed fallback); no neutral third-party validation/FDA clearance (same as Vera/OpenEvidence — say so); license landmines uncleared (StatPearls NC-ND, SNOMED/UMLS India, NICE); bio-age error bars real (directional, not diagnostic); India peptide/Rx regulatory exposure (keep clinician-gated); clinician app underbuilt in places (the two-sided moat depends on finishing it); prod on a fragile 512MB box (use-of-funds, not a weakness to hide).
