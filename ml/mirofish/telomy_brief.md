# Telomy: a longevity and preventive-health app for India (product brief for simulated customer feedback)

## What it is
Telomy keeps all of a person's health information in one private "Vault":
- lab reports and imaging, read automatically and checked;
- wearable data and daily habits.

From that it:
- estimates their **longevity age** (PhenoAge from a routine blood panel);
- predicts 10- and 30-year disease risk with validated equations (AHA PREVENT, diabetes, fatty liver, kidney);
- gives a daily **Recovery, Sleep and Strain** score from a chest ECG band worn at night.

An AI assistant, **Sinc**, answers questions using only the person's own records and shows its evidence. Doctors and partner wellness centres can see the same data with the person's consent.

## What the user actually does
- Uploads a PDF blood report → Telomy extracts the values and shows which are optimal, in range or out of range.
- Wears a light ECG chest band to bed → in the morning the app shows:
  - time asleep and the 4 sleep stages (light, deep, REM, awake);
  - lowest heart rate, HRV and breathing rate;
  - a Recovery score with advice ("Pace yourself").
- During the day the band (if worn) shows stress, strain and live heart rate. Users log meals, supplements, caffeine, alcohol, smoking and other events.
- At partner centres (cold plunge, sauna, red light, compression, IV drips), users record sessions. Telomy measures how their own body responds.
- Screens the heart rhythm for atrial-fibrillation patterns (a screening flag, not a diagnosis).
- Can book a doctor and get a monthly report signed by a doctor.

## What is different
- **Medical-grade single-lead ECG** in the band, not an optical wrist sensor. Every algorithm is validated on public hospital datasets, and the accuracy is published.
- **Everything is explained.** Every number shows its source and method. Medical claims stay wellness-only.
- **On-device and private.** Sleep stages, rhythm screening and scores run on the phone.

## Known limits (told to users)
- Sleep stages from ECG agree with a hospital sleep study about two-thirds of the time.
- The band has no motion, temperature or SpO₂ sensor.
- iPhone only today (Android later).
- Not a medical device; it does not diagnose.
